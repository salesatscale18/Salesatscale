FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Sales at Scale for?

B2B companies that have found early traction and now need a structured, repeatable way to grow revenue — typically founder-led teams moving to their first sales hires, or teams whose growth has plateaued.

Do you do the selling for us?

Our focus is building the system and the team so you own your growth long term. Where it helps, we can run parts of the motion with you while your team ramps.

How long does an engagement take?

It depends on scope. A focused diagnostic can take a few weeks; a full build-out usually runs over a few months. We'll recommend a scope after the first call.

What happens on the strategy call?

We'll talk about your goals, where things are getting stuck and whether we're a good fit. You'll leave with at least a few concrete ideas — no obligation.

Ready to scale your sales?

Tell us a bit about your business and we'll get back to you to set up a free strategy call.
